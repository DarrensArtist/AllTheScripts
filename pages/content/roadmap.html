Publishing rule

A project moves into the main library only when the page can teach something real.

That means a clear summary, honest status, useful breakdown, and enough direction for someone else to recreate or learn from it. The roadmap exists so unfinished work can stay visible without lowering quality.

What to prioritise
  • Finish one complete Pong lesson page with build and walkthrough.
  • Add screenshots or captures to non-playable project pages once prototypes exist.
  • Tie scripts and templates back to the specific projects they support.
