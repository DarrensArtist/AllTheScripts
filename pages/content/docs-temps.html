Documents & Templates

Production helpers that support the messy middle of development.

These resources are aimed at the parts of game development that often get skipped until they become a problem: scope, schedules, budgets, handoff notes, and pitch structure.

Pitch & Progress Pack

How To Use This Section

Start here if you need structure before you need more code.

The templates are intended to shorten setup time and reduce project drift. They are most useful when paired with a real prototype, which is why the games and scripts sections stay nearby.
