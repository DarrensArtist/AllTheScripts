About The Site

Built to make game-dev work feel more tangible.

All The Scripts is aimed at small teams, solo developers, and hobbyists who want a clearer path from idea to playable build. The site focuses on useful outputs instead of broad theory: working prototypes, reusable logic, and documents that help projects stay organized.

Who it is for

Developers who want to learn by adapting real pieces of work instead of starting every file from zero.

  • Solo developers building first commercial or hobby projects
  • Indie teams needing quick planning and production structure
  • Creators exploring mechanics through small contained builds

What is published here

The site is organized around assets that directly help a project move forward.

  • Playable experiments that prove out a mechanic
  • Templates for planning, budgeting, and pitching
  • Scripts and breakdowns that can be reused or studied

How the site is developing

The current version is intentionally focused on a small number of sections that are actually usable.

  • Navigation now centers on finished public areas
  • Prototype pages are tied more clearly to the resources around them
  • Future categories will only go live when they have real content
Working Principle

Publish fewer things, but make them easier to use.

A short list of dependable pages is better than a larger menu filled with placeholders. That principle now guides the site structure: only real destinations are in the main nav.

Best next step
  • Open the games page if you want a playable entry point.
  • Open the documents page if you need production support first.
  • Open the scripts page to see the direction for reusable code.
