About

I’m Darren. I build games, systems and practical ways to move ideas forward.

AllTheScripts is the focused home for my game-development work: substantial projects, useful prototypes and the workflows I refine while making them.

What I focus on

I work with Unity across game ideas, gameplay systems, prototyping and browser deployment. I’m interested in making individual parts understandable, testing them in useful slices and building toward stronger complete experiences.

I use AI-assisted development deliberately: to structure work, explore implementation paths, maintain documentation and validate outcomes. The decisions, direction and judgement remain mine, and the work shown here is the evidence.

Why AllTheScripts exists

It gives this work a clear home without pretending to be a studio or a team. The site can stay complete with a few pieces and grow only when I have something worth showing.
